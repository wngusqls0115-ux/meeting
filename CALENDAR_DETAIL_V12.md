# V12 — Large F/U Calendar

- Left sidebar calendar keeps its compact view.
- Click `F/U 캘린더 · 확대 보기` or the month label to open the calendar in the right/main pane.
- Opening a meeting hides the calendar detail and shows the meeting detail, matching the existing navigation model.
- Large calendar shows:
  - previous/current/next month navigation
  - current month summary
  - total/open/completed F/U counts
  - F/U items inside each date
  - selected-date F/U list
  - F/U memo dialog on item click
- All V10/V11 locked features remain required.
